What JunkMint Does

From lead to ledger. Purpose-built.

Every feature exists because junk removal demands it. No bloat, no workarounds, no features you'll never touch.

01

Photo-to-Quote AI

Customer sends a photo of their junk. AI estimates volume, suggests a price from your rate card, and sends a branded quote -- in under 60 seconds.

02

Truck Load Tracker

Visual gauge shows estimated fill level after each pickup. When it hits your threshold, the route auto-inserts a dump run at the nearest facility.

03

Dump Fee Tracking

Crew logs weight tickets and fees at the dump. You see true per-job cost: revenue minus labor, fuel, and disposal. Real margins, not guesses.

04

On-Site Change Orders

"While you're here, grab the garage too." Crew adjusts the job, customer approves the new price by text. No awkward conversations, no disputes.

05

Smart Route Builder

Sequences your stops by proximity, accounts for dump facility locations, and estimates when your truck will fill. Routes that actually match how you work.

06

Automated Follow-Ups

Quote follow-ups, review requests, re-service nudges -- all on autopilot. Configurable timing, your voice, TCPA compliant. Never lose a lead to silence.

07

Card on File at Booking

No card, no booking. Auto-charge on job completion or crew-initiated tap-to-charge. Zero accounts receivable. Junk removal should be a cash-flow business.

08

Donation Receipts

Crew drops items at Goodwill? App generates a branded receipt with items and estimated value. Emailed to the customer automatically for their tax deduction.
